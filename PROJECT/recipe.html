Delicious Strawberry Custard

Easy to make, absolutely delicious

Ingredients

  • 2 cups milk
  • 3 tbsp custard powder
  • 4 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/4 cup water
  • 2 cups fresh strawberries, sliced

Instructions

  1. Mix custard powder with 1/4 cup water to form a smooth paste
  2. Heat milk in a saucepan over medium heat
  3. Add sugar and stir until dissolved
  4. Add custard paste slowly, whisking continuously
  5. Cook for 2-3 minutes until thickened
  6. Add vanilla extract and remove from heat
  7. Cool to room temperature, then refrigerate for 1 hour
  8. Layer strawberries and custard in bowls and serve

Tips

  • Use half cream and half milk for extra richness
  • Add rose water or lemon zest for variation
  • Blend some strawberries into the custard for pink color
  • Use an extra tablespoon of custard powder for thicker consistency
  • Make ahead 2-3 hours and refrigerate
