Delicious Fried Rice

Quick, Easy, and Tasty

Ingredients

  • 3 cups cooked rice (day-old, cooled)
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup mixed vegetables (peas, carrots, corn)
  • 150g chicken or shrimp (optional)
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp oyster sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 2 green onions, chopped
  • Salt & pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a wok or large pan over high heat. Add garlic and cook for 30 seconds
  2. Add chicken/shrimp if using and cook until done. Remove and set aside
  3. Add 1 tbsp oil, pour in beaten eggs, scramble until cooked. Remove and set aside
  4. Add remaining oil, stir-fry mixed vegetables for 2-3 minutes
  5. Add cooled rice, break up clumps, and stir-fry for 3-4 minutes
  6. Add soy sauce, oyster sauce, and sesame oil. Mix well
  7. Return cooked eggs and protein to the pan and toss together
  8. Add green onions, cook for 1-2 minutes, season with salt and pepper, and serve hot

Tips

  • Use day-old or cooled rice - fresh rice gets mushy
  • High heat is essential for authentic wok flavor
  • Scramble eggs separately first for fluffy pieces
  • Make sure each rice grain is coated with oil
  • Customize with cashews, pineapple, broccoli, or mushrooms
  • For spicy version, add chili paste or sriracha
