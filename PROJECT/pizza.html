Delicious Homemade Pizza

Crispy Crust, Fresh Toppings, Pure Perfection

Ingredients

  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tbsp active dry yeast
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 cup warm water
  • 1 cup pizza sauce
  • 2 cups mozzarella cheese, shredded
  • 2 medium tomatoes, sliced
  • 1/2 cup fresh basil leaves
  • Salt & pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Mix warm water, sugar, and yeast in a bowl. Let sit for 5 minutes until foamy
  2. Add flour, salt, and olive oil. Mix until a dough forms
  3. Knead the dough on a floured surface for 8-10 minutes until smooth and elastic
  4. Place dough in an oiled bowl, cover with a damp cloth, and let rise for 1-1.5 hours until doubled in size
  5. Preheat oven to 475°F (245°C). If using a pizza stone, place it in the oven
  6. Punch down the dough and shape it into a circle on a pizza pan or stone
  7. Spread pizza sauce over the dough, leaving 1/2 inch at the edges. Add mozzarella cheese generously
  8. Arrange tomato slices and fresh basil on top. Drizzle with olive oil and season with salt and pepper
  9. Bake for 12-15 minutes until the crust is golden and cheese is melted and browned

Tips

  • Use warm water around 110°F (43°C) for best yeast activation
  • Knead the dough well for 8-10 minutes to develop gluten and make it elastic
  • Don't skip the rising time - this makes the pizza light and fluffy
  • Use a pizza stone for a crispier crust and preheat it for 30 minutes
  • Add fresh basil after baking to keep the flavor vibrant
  • For extra flavor, use a mix of mozzarella and parmesan cheese
